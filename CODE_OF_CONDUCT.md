# Code of conduct

Compyla follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

Harassment and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or the wiki.
To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@iChintanSoni](https://github.com/iChintanSoni),
through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
